`efficient-fable`). Subagent output is evidence, not a verdict — same
discipline as `icarus-critic`.

**Fold or new.** `[Hypothesis]` only: fold into `workflow-design` surface
assignment and spawn rules. Graduate only if fellow sessions show frontier
