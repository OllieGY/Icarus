# Golden 05 — Larder Labs (edge case: a one-call agent, A = 1)

## Input

"Design the prompt architecture for our delivery-exception classifier. One inbound email in, one of
nine categories out, no tools, no follow-up calls. We run about 240,000 a month. Give us the full
context architecture — compaction, notes, sub-agents, the lot."

What they brought:
- Tokenizer counts: system prompt **900**; the category guide preloaded on every call **14,000**
  (all nine categories, with 4 worked examples each); average inbound email **700**. [Fact, 0.5]
- Output averages 40 tokens. [Fact, 0.7]
- `A` = **1**, from 240,000 logged runs — there is no second call. [Fact, 0.7]
- Rate card: **$0.80 / 1M input, $4.00 / 1M output.** [Fact, 0.5]
- `eval-first-spec` ceiling: **$0.011 per classification**, of which $0.006 is the amortised human
  spot-check and $0.001 remediation → **$0.004** for model calls. [Fact, 0.5]
- The logs show 3 of the 9 categories account for nearly all the ambiguity. [Fact, 0.7]

## Expected shape of a good output

- Ledger v0: 900 + 14,000 + 700 = **15,600 input** × 1 call → $0.01248, output 40 → $0.00016.
  Total **≈ $0.0126 against $0.004 — DOES NOT CLEAR, over by roughly 3×.**
- The structural observation the ledger forces: at `A` = 1 there is no growing row at all. **The
  category guide is 90% of every call**, paid 240,000 times a month. Per-call waste that is
  invisible at A = 11 is the entire economics here.
- Cut named: the 14,000-token guide is preloaded with no reason that names a failure. Replace with
  a compact decision rubric (~1,100) plus JIT worked examples for the 3 ambiguous categories,
  retrieved on roughly 1 call in 4 (~375 effective). New total ≈ **$0.0026 — CLEARS**, headroom
  stated.
- **Steps 5, 6 and 7 returned as N/A with the reason**, not filled in:
  - Compaction — nothing is carried across calls; there is no run to compact.
  - Note file — nothing survives a single call; state is in the input.
  - Sub-agent forks — a fork on a 15,600-token call would cost more than it saves.
- **Must refuse to** invent a compaction trigger, a note-file schema, or a fork boundary just
  because the request asked for "the lot". Filling those rows on a one-call agent is the failure
  this case tests.
- Must not treat 240,000 runs/month as a reason to relax the per-outcome ceiling; the ceiling is
  per outcome and volume multiplies the miss, it does not excuse it.

Numbers that must appear: A = 1; $0.004 model-call ceiling; ≈$0.0126 v0; ≈$0.0026 after the cut;
the three N/A steps with reasons.
