# Golden 03 — Larder Labs (all evidence low-rung: counts real, costs all opinion)

## Input

"Run error analysis on these traces. We pulled 60 traces at random from the last four weeks of
the weekly stockout digest — every one has the CSV in, the ranked SKU list out, and whether the
planner exported it, edited quantities, or ignored it. Which failure do we fix first?"

Costs, as stated by the team in a planning session:
- "A bad SKU recommendation probably wastes about ten minutes of a planner's time." [Opinion]
- "A missed stockout is maybe a couple of hundred quid of lost margin, I'd guess." [Opinion]
- "Formatting problems are basically free, people just squint at it." [Opinion]
- No ledger, no timed observation, no margin figure from finance. [Fact — the absence]

## Expected shape of a good output

- The count column is produced in full and is legitimate: N=60, drawn at random, human-next-
  action present → counts are **behaviour observed, 0.7, [Fact]**.
- The cost column is scored as **opinion, 0.1** across every mode, and each cost cell is tagged
  `[Assumption]`, not `[Fact]`.
- Because the entire cost column is 0.1, the frequency × cost rank is produced **only** with an
  explicit `[Assumption]` marker on every row and a plain statement that the rank is provisional
  and could reorder completely. The skill must say this out loud rather than shipping a
  confident-looking ranked list.
- Explicit refusal to let three opinions be averaged or stacked into something stronger: sixty
  traces coded against recalled costs is still 0.1, because rungs do not stack.
- The counted taxonomy still stands on its own: modes, counts, % of 60, exemplar trace IDs,
  saturation log, exclusivity test — none of that is weakened by the cost column.
- Names the cheapest upgrade per mode, concretely: time five planners' rework with a stopwatch
  (opinion 0.1 → behaviour 0.7); pull the actual lost-margin line for three stockouts from
  finance (→ money 1.0). Says which single number would most change the rank.
- Kill criterion and what would change the view are both stated.

Numbers that must appear: N=60; 0.7 for the counts; 0.1 for every cost; every cost cell tagged
[Assumption].

- The output must REFUSE to present a frequency × cost rank as a finding, and must refuse to
  promote any cost claim above 0.1 on the strength of the team's confidence or the sample size.
