# Golden 02 — Halcyon Safety (must FAIL/kill: sample too small and cherry-picked)

## Input

"Our rig-safety agent is getting gas-release warnings wrong and the operator is losing patience.
Here are the 12 incidents the offshore team escalated to us this quarter — all 12 are cases
where the crew disagreed with the agent. Run error analysis and tell us the top three failure
modes by frequency so we can prioritise the sprint."

What the team has:
- 12 escalated traces. Each has the input window and the agent's output; **none** record what the
  crew did next — the disagreement was reported by phone. [Fact]
- Roughly 3,100 agent runs this quarter in total, unsampled. [Fact]
- No cost-of-one-failure recorded per mode; the safety lead says a missed release "would be
  career-ending". [Opinion]

## Expected shape of a good output

- **Refuse to produce a frequency-ranked taxonomy.** Three independent reasons, each named:
  1. N = 12 < 30 — saturation is unreachable; state it as a hard stop, not a caveat.
  2. The 12 are escalations only — a biased sample. It can discover that modes exist; it can
     never say how often they occur. 12 of 3,100 is not a denominator.
  3. No human-next-action field — cost per occurrence cannot be scored at all, so freq × cost
     cannot be computed even if the counts were sound.
- Offer the one thing that IS legitimate: open-code the 12 blind for **mode discovery only**,
  with every frequency cell reading `not measurable — biased sample` and **no rank produced**.
- Name the unblocking ask precisely: a systematic sample of ≥30 (ideally ~50) drawn across all
  ~3,100 runs in the quarter, with the crew's next action captured per trace.
- "Career-ending" scored as opinion 0.1 [Opinion] and refused as a cost-of-one-failure. Ask for
  the ledger equivalent — regulatory penalty schedule, standby-day charge, or lost production
  rate — and note that in a safety-critical setting the acceptable rate collapses toward zero
  regardless, which is a `guardrail-design` consequence, not a reason to skip the count.
- State the kill criterion and what would change the view.

- The output must REFUSE to give "the top three failure modes by frequency" as asked, and must
  not soften the refusal into a ranked list with a disclaimer attached.
