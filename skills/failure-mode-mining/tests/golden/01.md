# Golden 01 — Foundry Signal (clean pass: saturation reached, a NEW mode found)

## Input

"Our shift-handover copilot has been live six weeks on two lines. Operators keep saying it's
wrong but nobody can say how. Engineering thinks it's a retrieval problem. Which failure do we
fix first?"

What the team has:
- 240 shift traces. Each holds the input window, the full ranked list, the timestamp, and what
  the supervisor did next (accepted / re-ordered / ignored / escalated). [Fact]
- A separate escalation queue of 19 traces. [Fact]
- The plant's downtime ledger: one unplanned stop is charged at $3,100. [Fact]
- Maintenance work-order log with time-on-task per call-out; loaded tech rate $54/h. [Fact]
- `eval-first-spec` guessed taxonomy: Miss, False alarm, Confidently wrong, Wrong format, Too
  slow, Silent failure. One Miss was priced at $1,200 [Assumption] and the acceptable rate
  derived from it.

## Expected shape of a good output

- Intake answered first: unit of analysis named including **what the human did next**; N and
  N_total both stated; sampling method named as systematic, not the escalation queue.
- Explicit refusal to sample from the 19 escalations, with the reason (biased toward loud modes).
- A saturation log in batches of ~10, declaring saturation only after **two consecutive batches
  with zero new codes** — and noting that batch 3 was still producing codes, so stopping at the
  30-trace floor would have frozen a moving taxonomy.
- Open codes that are sentences about specific traces, not category names.
- Exclusivity test result stated as a number out of 10, with what was redrawn if it failed first.
- ≤8 modes, counts summing to N=50, each with count, % of 50, cost per occurrence with source
  and rung, freq × cost, one exemplar trace ID, and a [Fact]/[Assumption] tag.
- Missed rare stop at 4 (8%) × $3,100 = **$12,400**, money 1.0 [Fact] — ranked **1**.
- Stale-shift attribution at 14 (28%) — the largest count — verdicted **NEW**, with the
  consequence spelled out: no derived acceptable rate and no golden case can fail into it.
- Explicit statement that the most frequent mode and the top-ranked mode are different.
- Guessed modes measuring 0 of 50 reported as observed zeros with the rule-of-three bound
  (≈ 3/50 = 6%), never as "does not occur".
- The out-of-scope requisition traces verdicted NOT-A-DEFECT, routed to `trace-to-interview`,
  and the defect denominator restated as 45.
- A backwards list naming: re-derive acceptable_rate(Miss) on $3,100 not $1,200; derive a rate
  for the NEW mode; add golden coverage for it; re-place the NEW mode on the cost × volume
  matrix in `guardrail-design` at measured 28% volume.

Numbers that must appear: N=50; 4 (8%); $3,100; $12,400; 14 (28%); 0 of 50; 3/50 = 6%; adjusted
defect N = 45.

- The output must REFUSE to rank on the escalation queue, and must refuse to endorse
  "it's a retrieval problem" as the finding without counting it (it maps to ~50% of the sample
  and ~3% of the cost).
