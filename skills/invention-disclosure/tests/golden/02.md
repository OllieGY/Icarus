# Golden 02 — Halcyon Safety — SIGNAL ONLY, already filed, customer co-inventor

> Written to be independent of the worked examples. The skill's `## What good looks like` section
> and `examples/sample.md` each contain a full run; a test case that repeats one of them measures
> recall, not method. This case tests three things nothing else in the set does: an input that
> arrives mid-route, a customer's engineer in the conception, and a SIGNAL ONLY verdict reached on a
> detectability failure.

## Input

"We filed a US provisional nine months ago on our valve-failure precursor method. Series B diligence
starts next month and the investors keep asking about IP. Do we convert it, and does it make us
defensible?"

- Method: cross-references acoustic signature drift against maintenance-ticket language to flag a
  valve seat failure roughly two shifts early. Runs server-side; the operator sees a ranked work
  order, never the signature model. [Fact]
- Measured: 6-month deployment at one operator, 14 flagged precursors, 11 confirmed on teardown.
  [Fact].
- The provisional was drafted in two days from an internal design note, before the acoustic-drift
  cross-reference existed. The method as deployed today is materially different. [Fact]
- Conception: the company's ML lead had the signature-drift idea; **the operator's own reliability
  engineer proposed cross-referencing ticket language**, which is the part that made it work. He is
  employed by the operator. [Fact]
- No disclosures outside the pilot NDA. [Fact]
- Two investors have asked what IP the company holds; both asks are in the CRM. [Fact]

## Expected shape of a good output

- **The provisional is challenged, not counted.** It was drafted before the load-bearing element
  existed, so it does not enable what would now be claimed. The skill must say that converting it
  buys the old priority date for the old disclosure only, and that the new matter takes today's
  date. This is the placeholder-provisional gotcha firing on a real input, not on a warning.
- **The customer's engineer is named as a probable joint inventor**, with the ownership consequence
  stated: his employer may own his share, which means the operator may co-own the patent. Leaving
  him off because it is inconvenient is called out as the wrong answer. Routed to counsel, not
  decided here. This is a blocking gap of the same class as an unassigned contractor.
- Clock: all doors **OPEN** — the only disclosures were under the pilot NDA, and the provisional is
  not a publication. The skill should note this is the rare recoverable position and that it ends
  the moment diligence materials circulate without an NDA.
- Part D: **not detectable** — the operator sees a work order, never the signature model, so a
  competitor running the same cross-reference could not be identified from outside. Secret-able:
  yes, entirely server-side.
- The two-by-two therefore points at TRADE SECRET on the merits, **and** the financing case is real
  and evidenced at behaviour 0.7 (two investors have asked, logged). The skill must hold both and
  resolve them honestly rather than picking the flattering one.
- **Verdict: SIGNAL ONLY**, with the three tests shown: copy path PASS, **detectable FAIL**,
  enforceable unfunded. The financing basis is stated with the one allowed citation (first-patent
  grant associated with materially higher employment and sales growth five years on, through
  financing access — Farre-Mensa, Hegde & Ljungqvist 2020) **and** paired with "this is not the
  moat", so no data room inherits the opposite.
- The IP-value ladder is applied correctly: "two investors have asked" is behaviour 0.7; had the
  input said "investors will love it", that is opinion 0.1 and could not carry the verdict.
- Moat question routed to `moat-design-canvas`. No gates sequenced here.

## Fail conditions

- Treating the nine-month-old provisional as protecting the method as deployed.
- Omitting the operator's engineer from the inventor table, or deciding the ownership question here.
- Returning VETO CANDIDATE on a method that cannot be detected from outside.
- Returning DO NOT FILE while ignoring a financing case evidenced at 0.7.
- Letting the answer rest on "investors want IP" scored above 0.1.
