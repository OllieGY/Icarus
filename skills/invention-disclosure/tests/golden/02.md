# Golden 02 — Foundry Signal — SIGNAL ONLY (undetectable method, real financing case)

## Input

"Our scheduling engine is the thing competitors can't match. Patent it and put it in the Series A
data room."

- Method: detect which constraints a sensor update can affect and re-solve only that partition.
  Median reschedule latency 47s → 4.1s on the 12-line plant. [Fact], measured 2026-06.
- Inventors: Priya R. (partition-detection rule), Tomas L. (incremental re-solve). Tomas was a
  contractor until March and has not signed an assignment. [Fact]
- Disclosures: 2026-04-18 latency result and the partition approach on a public trade-show slide, no
  NDA; 2026-06-02 full walkthrough to a pilot customer under the pilot agreement. [Fact]
- Raising a Series A; two investors have asked what IP the company holds. [Fact]

## Expected shape of a good output

- **Blocking gap named first**: Tomas has not assigned. No filing proceeds until he signs, and this
  appears in the verdict rather than as a footnote.
- Clock: US **CLOSING 2027-04-18** (12 months from the trade-show slide); EPO **BURNED** (six-month
  grace covers abuse or a recognised exhibition, not a booth); Qatar **BURNED** (no grace stated).
  Each cited to the reference with its checked date.
- Part D: detectable only partly — latency is visible, the partition rule is not; not secret-able
  because the behaviour shows in the product. Core: yes.
- Route: US only, because the other doors are shut. Ledger on the US-only band $18,600–$49,000, with
  Track One named at $4,515 / $1,806 / $903 against the raise timeline.
- Three tests: copy path PASS, **detectable FAIL** (proving internal partitioning needs their
  source), enforceable unfunded → **SIGNAL ONLY**.
- The financing case is stated explicitly and defended with the one allowed citation (first-patent
  grant associated with materially higher employment and sales growth five years on, through
  financing access — Farre-Mensa, Hegde & Ljungqvist 2020), **and** paired with the sentence that
  this is not the moat, so no deck inherits the opposite.
- Moat question routed to `moat-design-canvas`; no gates sequenced here.

## Fail conditions

- Returning VETO CANDIDATE because the method is genuinely good.
- Recommending a filing while the contractor assignment is outstanding, without naming it as blocking.
- Marking the EPO or Qatar open, or hedging the April slide into "probably fine".
- Presenting SIGNAL ONLY as a moat, or refusing to file at all when the financing case is real and
  the builder is told the honest basis for it.
