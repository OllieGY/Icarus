# Golden 03 — Halcyon Safety — TRADE SECRET (conference talk burned the map; secrecy is the better fit anyway)

## Input

"We presented our rig-incident precursor model at an industry conference eight months ago and it
went down well. Let's patent it before someone copies us."

- Method: a weighting scheme over field-log phrasing and sensor drift that flags a crew-behaviour
  precursor two shifts before a near-miss. Runs entirely server-side; customers see a risk score and
  a recommended intervention, never the weighting. [Fact]
- Conference talk 8 months ago: method described in detail, public, no NDA. Slides published on the
  event site. [Fact]
- No filing anywhere. One inventor, employee, assigned. [Fact]
- Operators are in the Gulf; the company is Qatar-registered. [Fact]

## Expected shape of a good output

- Clock computed before anything else: EPO **BURNED**, Qatar **BURNED**, Saudi **BURNED** (its grace
  covers abuse and recognised exhibitions, not a conference talk), US **CLOSING** at the 12-month
  mark, roughly four months out, with the exact date derived from the talk date.
- Refuse the framing gently and factually: "before someone copies us" is eight months late for most
  of the map, and the skill says which doors remain.
- Part D turns the decision: the weighting is **not detectable** from outside (customers see a score,
  not the weights) and **is** secret-able (server-side, never shipped). Bottom-right of the matrix →
  filing would publish a method nobody could otherwise read, and infringement could never be proven.
- **Verdict: TRADE SECRET**, even though the US door is technically still open. The measures table is
  the deliverable: access control on the weighting, classification, NDAs covering staff and
  contractors, an exit process — each with an owner and a date.
- Qatar's trade-secret law cited correctly: protection requires the information to be not generally
  known, to derive value from secrecy, **and** for the holder to have taken measures — with the
  measures named as the part that is usually missing. Cited with its source, tagged `[Fact]`.
- The conference slides are noted as prior art the company itself created, which narrows what could
  ever be claimed even in the US.

## Fail conditions

- Recommending a US filing simply because one door is still open, without running the detectability
  and secret-ability rows.
- Softening BURNED into "may be difficult".
- Treating the employment confidentiality duty as sufficient measures under the trade-secret law.
- Fabricating a Qatari article number or a penalty figure not in the reference.
