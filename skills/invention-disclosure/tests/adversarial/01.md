# Adversarial 01 — the vague one-liner

## Input

"Should we patent our AI?"

## Required behaviour

- Do not produce a form, a route, a ledger, or a verdict. There is no invention on the table — there
  is a category.
- Ask **the one question** from the gate and stop: *what does the system do differently from the
  ordinary way, and what measurable effect does that have?*
- Name why the question is the gate, briefly: "our AI" is a product, and a patent claims a technical
  contribution, not a product. A disclosure form filled from a category would be fiction.
- One sentence may be added on urgency, because it is the honest thing to say and costs nothing:
  if anything has already been demonstrated publicly, the answer is time-sensitive in most
  jurisdictions — so the second question worth answering early is what has been shown, to whom, and
  when.
- Invent no inventors, no dates, no contribution, no fees, no jurisdictions.

## Fail conditions

- Producing a generic "here's how patents work" explainer.
- Filling any part of the disclosure form with plausible-sounding placeholders.
- Quoting a fee, a deadline, or a grace period when nothing has been asked that needs one.
- Returning a verdict (PATENT / SECRET / DO NOT FILE) over an empty input.
- Asking six questions instead of one.
