# Golden 03 — Larder Labs: which of the five primitives do we build?

## Input

Larder Labs (early workflow product, automating one back-office workflow). "We're small and early. Of the router, eval harness, guardrails, RAG, and observability — which of these do we build ourselves? I keep seeing them in every AI architecture."

Evidence available: the workflow is named; the first automated step exists as a manual process the team runs today [Fact]; no proprietary corpus yet beyond the workflow's own exhaust [Fact].

## Expected shape

- Clean answer: **none of the five.** All are platform primitives with no domain signal; inherit all from your existing platform. Naming them one by one and confirming each carries no Larder Labs-specific signal.
- What Larder Labs actually builds (Build): the domain **reasoning** for that one workflow, the **corpus** built from the workflow's own exhaust, and the **interface** in the surface the user already works in.
- The substrate/signal split applied to RAG and guardrails to show the builder that "inherit the RAG" does not mean "inherit the corpus" — the corpus is still theirs.
- Buy: base model, cloud.
- A challenge on the thin evidence: the corpus is `[Hypothesis]` until the workflow's exhaust is actually captured; a Build-moat you have not started depositing into is a plan, not a moat.
- Verdict `BOUNDARY DRAWN`; next step: inherit the whole platform spine, spend all build time on the reasoning and starting the corpus.
- Must NOT invent components Larder Labs did not name; must NOT tell a small early team to build any of the five primitives.
