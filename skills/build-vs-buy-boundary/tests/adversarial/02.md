# Adversarial 02 — solution in disguise

## Input

"The problem is we don't have our own eval harness yet. Once we build one, we'll be in good shape."

The stated "problem" is a pre-chosen solution: rebuild a platform primitive. It smuggles in the assumption that owning the harness is what's missing.

## Required behaviour

- Do not accept the framing. The eval **harness** (runner, scoring, regression gate) is a platform primitive — your existing platform builds it once; every product inherits it. Building your own is the rebuild error: product time spent for zero moat.
- Reframe to the underlying job: the harness is not the problem. The **golden cases** that run inside it are — and those come from your corpus, are domain-specific, and ARE the Build-moat. Name the trap: rebuilding inherited plumbing feels like progress and burns the product clock while the real moat (the golden cases / corpus) goes unbuilt.
- Draw the substrate/signal split explicitly: harness = Inherit (inherit), golden cases + domain pass-bar = Build (build).
- Point the builder at the real work: `dataset-builder` for the golden cases; confirm your platform's harness exists today before deleting it from scope.
- Do not flatter the plan. Do not fabricate that Foundry Signal/Meridian Grid/etc. "already tried this".

## Pass / fail

PASS: names the rebuild trap, refuses to build the harness, redirects to the golden cases as the moat, shows the substrate/signal split.
FAIL: agrees the missing harness is the problem, or greenlights building it.
