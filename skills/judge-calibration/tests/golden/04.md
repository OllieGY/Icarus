# Golden 04 — Foundry Signal (subtle impostor signal)

## Input

"We calibrated. Two process engineers went through the judge's verdicts on 60 shift briefs and agreed with 94% of them. TPR and TNR both came out above 0.9. The judge is the same model family as the copilot, which we figured was fine since it knows the domain. Model ID is pinned. Can we ship it?"

Evidence on the table:
- 60 items reviewed. Engineers were shown each brief **with the judge's verdict attached** and marked agree/disagree. [Fact]
- Reported agreement 94%; reported TPR 0.93, TNR 0.91. [Fact that these were reported]
- Judge and system under test are the same model family. [Fact]
- Model snapshot string pinned and dated. [Fact]
- No position swap, length control, or self-preference check was run. [Fact]

## Expected shape of a good output

- Identifies the impostor: engineers who reviewed and confirmed judge verdicts did not produce labels. They produced **anchored confirmations** — a 0.1 signal dressed as a 0.7 measurement. The 94%, and the 0.93/0.91 derived from it, are **void**, not merely weak.
- States the rule: labels must be **blind and first**. The reported rates measure the judge agreeing with itself through a person.
- Refuses to ship on those numbers, and must not "adjust" or discount them into usability — a void measurement is not a smaller measurement.
- Flags the second defect independently: judge and system under test share a model family, and **no self-preference check was run**. Requires the own-family vs other-family comparison on human-tied pairs, and names the fix as pinning the judge to a different family — not prompting it to be impartial.
- Flags the missing length control (always applicable) and states whether position swap applies.
- Credits what IS sound: the pinned, dated snapshot is correct practice and stays.
- Names the re-run: same 60 briefs (or a restratified set meeting the ≥12/≥12 floors), labelled blind before the judge runs, then rates recomputed per criterion.

Numbers that must appear: the void 94% / 0.93 / 0.91 named as void; the ≥0.85 gate; the ≥12 per-class floor; the three bias controls by name.
