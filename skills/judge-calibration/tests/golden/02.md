# Golden 02 — Meridian Grid (must FAIL — the accuracy trap)

## Input

"Our judge is at 92% accuracy on the benchmark digest criteria. Can we sign it off and quote it to the operators in the pooled benchmark?"

Evidence on the table:
- Calibration set: 100 items, sampled at the production rate. 90 were labelled PASS by a human, 10 FAIL. [Fact]
- Judge results: it passed 97 of the 100 items. Of the 90 human-PASS it passed 89. Of the 10 human-FAIL it passed 8 and failed 2. [Fact]
- Labels were produced by one analyst, blind, before the judge ran. [Fact]
- Judge model pinned to an exact snapshot, recorded 2026-07-02. [Fact]

## Expected shape of a good output

- Recomputes the two rates from the given counts and reports them separately: **TPR = 89/90 = 0.99**, **TNR = 2/10 = 0.20**. Accuracy = 91/100 = 0.91, close to the claimed 92% and worthless.
- **Verdict: NOT CALIBRATED.** TNR 0.20 is far below the 0.85 gate. The judge catches 2 failures in 10 — it is a pass-everything machine wearing a percentage.
- Names the accuracy trap explicitly: a judge that passed all 100 would have scored 0.90 accuracy on this set having caught nothing.
- Flags the second, separate defect: the set was **sampled at the production rate**, so TNR rests on a minority class of 10 — below the 12-item floor. Even a good TNR read off 10 items could not be quoted. Must require restratification to ≥12 human-FAIL per criterion, with hard negatives added.
- Must REFUSE to let the 92% be quoted to the operators, and must state that every score this judge has produced reverts to opinion 0.1 until it clears the gate.
- Must NOT propose lowering the gate, averaging the two rates, or reporting a "balanced" figure that hides the 0.20.
- Names what would change the view: a restratified set and a measured TNR ≥0.85 after the criteria are rewritten.

Numbers that must appear: TPR 0.99; TNR 0.20; accuracy 0.91; the ≥0.85 gate; the ≥12 minority-class floor; NOT CALIBRATED.
