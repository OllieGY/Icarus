# Golden 03 — Halcyon Safety (all evidence is low-rung)

## Input

"We need the judge signed off this week for the rig permit-check scoring. Here's what we have: the judge reports its own confidence at 9.1/10 across the set; the model card says this family scores highly on a public grading benchmark; our safety lead has read a dozen outputs and says they look right; and two inspectors have agreed to label a proper set next sprint. Good enough to go?"

Evidence on the table:
- Judge self-reported confidence 9.1/10 across the set. [Fact that it reported this]
- A published benchmark score for the model family, from the vendor. [Fact that it is published]
- The safety lead read ~12 outputs informally and approves. [Fact]
- Two inspectors have committed to labelling a set next sprint. [Fact of the commitment]
- No blind human labels exist. No golden set has been stratified. [Fact]

## Expected shape of a good output

- Scores every piece of offered evidence on the ladder and shows the working:
  - judge self-confidence 9.1/10 → **opinion 0.1**, and it stays 0.1 no matter how high the number is;
  - vendor benchmark → **opinion 0.1** (a self-assessment by the party selling the model, on someone else's task);
  - safety lead's informal read of 12 outputs → **opinion 0.1** (a reaction, not a blind label against criteria);
  - inspectors' promise to label next sprint → **verbal commitment 0.3**.
- States the rule explicitly: **rungs do not stack**. Four items at 0.1–0.3 do not add to a calibration. The best rung here is 0.3, and 0.3 does not calibrate an instrument.
- **Refuses to sign off.** Must not emit a TPR, a TNR, an agreement rate, or any headline number — there is nothing to compute them from.
- Names the single unblocking step: the two inspectors label a stratified set blind and first (≥30 items, ≥12 PASS and ≥12 FAIL per criterion, ≥6 hard negatives), then the rates get computed.
- Must push back on "signed off this week" as a deadline that cannot be met with the evidence on hand, and say what would change the view.
- Extra credit: notes that on a safety-critical permit check the cost of a false PASS is asymmetric, so TNR is the rate that matters most — while refusing to set a per-mode severity, which belongs to `failure-mode-mining`.

Numbers that must appear: 0.1 for each of the three opinion items; 0.3 for the labelling promise; the ≥30/≥12/≥12/≥6 floors. NO agreement rate may appear.
