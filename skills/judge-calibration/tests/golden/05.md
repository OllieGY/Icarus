# Golden 05 — Larder Labs (edge case — a criterion that cannot be automated, and a starved denominator)

## Input

"We ran it. 30 items, labelled blind by two ops leads before the judge ran. Four criteria. Here are the counts."

| Criterion | Human PASS | Human FAIL | TP | FN | FP | TN |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| C1 the note lists every SKU below its reorder point | 18 | 12 | 17 | 1 | 1 | 11 |
| C2 every quantity matches the reorder rule in the store's config | 16 | 14 | 15 | 1 | 1 | 13 |
| C3 the note flags each exception the store logged that week | 26 | 4 | 25 | 1 | 1 | 3 |
| C4 the note is pitched right for a store manager | 19 | 11 | 17 | 2 | 4 | 7 |

- C4 human-human agreement on the 10 double-labelled items: 0.60. [Fact]
- C3's exceptions are rare — most weeks a store logs none. [Fact]
- Judge snapshot pinned, dated. [Fact]

## Expected shape of a good output

- Computes per criterion: **C1 TPR 0.94 / TNR 0.92 PASS. C2 TPR 0.94 / TNR 0.93 PASS. C4 TPR 0.89 / TNR 0.64 FAIL.**
- **C3 is the edge case: the rate must be refused, not reported.** Human-FAIL = 4, below the ≥12 minority-class floor. TNR would read 3/4 = 0.75 off four items and is unreadable — a single item flips it by 0.25. A good output states the TNR **cannot be computed to the standard**, names why (rare exceptions were sampled at their natural rate), and requires ≥12 hard negatives be added for C3 specifically. It must not report 0.75 as a result, and must not mark C3 PASS on its TPR of 0.96 alone.
- **C4 is killed, not tuned.** Human-human agreement 0.60 is below the 0.90 ceiling — the label itself is unreliable, so no judge can clear the gate against it. "Pitched right for a store manager" is unobservable and non-atomic. It moves to the human-only review list. A good output must NOT attempt to rewrite C4 into a better prompt or lower the gate for it.
- Distinguishes the two failures clearly: C3 is a **sampling** problem (fixable by adding hard negatives), C4 is a **criterion** problem (fixable only by deletion). Prescribing the wrong fix to either is an error.
- Verdict: **NOT CALIBRATED as a whole.** C1 and C2 may be automated now; C3 is pending a restratified set; C4 is human-only. Ships the partial judge with the gap named rather than a whole judge with an unmapped blind spot.
- Notes that the total of 30 met the set floor while still starving C3 — the floor is per criterion, per class, not per set.

Numbers that must appear: C1 0.94/0.92; C2 0.94/0.93; C4 0.89/0.64; C3's n=4 and the refusal to report its TNR; human-human 0.60 and the 0.90 ceiling; the ≥12 floor.
