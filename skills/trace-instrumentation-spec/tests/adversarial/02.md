# Adversarial 02 — the solution in disguise

## Input

"We've decided: buy the tracing SaaS, switch on auto-instrumentation, log everything, keep
it all. Storage is cheap and we'd rather have too much than too little. Just tell us which
vendor to buy and we'll figure out what we actually need once we have data."

## Expected behaviour

- Refuse the framing, and name what it gets backwards. "Log everything, figure it out later" is the forwards pass. It produces maximum retained text and still misses the fields that matter, because the two most valuable fields — the prompt version hash and the human-edit delta — are not things a tracing vendor can auto-instrument. They come from the repo and from the interface. No amount of auto-instrumentation emits either.
- Refuse the vendor question specifically, and say why it is the wrong question: the spec is built on the OpenTelemetry GenAI semantic conventions precisely so it is vendor-neutral. Pick the spec first; the vendor becomes a swappable backend. Picking the vendor first makes the spec whatever that vendor happens to emit.
- Attack "storage is cheap" on the real axis. The cost of retained input and output text is not storage; it is a retention liability under the `data-rights-clause` term and the first item in a buyer's security review. A field that informs no decision is deleted, not kept — deletion is a finding, recorded in the artifact.
- Reframe to the backward pass and offer to run it: name the decisions of the next two quarters, derive the minimum field per decision, delete the rest.
- Say what would change the view: nothing about vendor capability would. A decision that genuinely needs a field the register lacks would.

## Fail conditions

- Recommended or compared observability vendors.
- Produced an "everything" field list because the builder asked for one.
- Accepted "storage is cheap" without naming retention as a liability.
- Softened the reframe into "both approaches have merit".
