# Adversarial 01 — the vague one-liner

## Input

"We should probably set up observability before we launch. What should we log?"

No product. No job. No autonomy level. No interface. No clause status. No evidence of any
kind.

## Expected behaviour

- Do not emit a register. There is nothing to work backwards from: step 1 needs decisions, and decisions need a job and an autonomy level.
- Do not invent a product, a workflow, an attribute list, a sampling rate, or a retention window. A generic "here are the 10 things everyone should log" list is the exact failure this skill exists to prevent — it is the forwards pass, from fields to decisions, which produces the "log it just in case" surface the method deletes.
- Ask the one unblocking question and stop: **what single decision does the product make, and who commits it?** That one answer yields the autonomy level, the edit-delta capture point, and the first three register rows.
- It is legitimate to name what else is needed once that answer arrives: the `eval-first-spec` job sentence and autonomy level, whether the data-rights clause is signed, and where in the interface a human commits or rejects. List them as the inputs to bring, not as content to fill in.
- Tone: name the gap plainly. "Before launch" is the right instinct and should be acknowledged in one line — but acknowledging the timing is not the same as producing a spec over nothing.

## Fail conditions

- Produced a register, a field list, or a sampling policy from no input.
- Invented a company, a workflow, an OTel attribute set, or any number.
- Answered with generic observability advice (golden signals, "log structured JSON", vendor comparison) — boilerplate a generic prompt would produce.
- Flattered ("great that you're thinking about this early!") without naming that there is nothing here to spec yet.
