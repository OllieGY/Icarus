# Golden 05 — Halcyon Safety (edge case: the latency band changes the pattern, not the level)

## Input

"The shift-handover draft agent is settled at L1 — the outgoing supervisor approves the note
before it posts to the incoming crew. But it's slow and people are abandoning it. What does
the user see while it works? Do we need to drop the approval step to make it feel fast?"

- Level L1, confirmed in `eval-first-spec`; the post is irreversible once the crew reads it.
  [Fact]
- Latency measured over 220 runs: p50 8s, p95 47s. The tail is the fan-out to 4 systems
  (permits, gas logs, maintenance queue, previous handover). [Fact] behaviour 0.7
- Abandonment: 31% of runs are closed before the draft appears; 84% of those abandons are on
  runs that exceeded 30s. [Fact] behaviour 0.7
- Supervisors start the draft on a tablet while walking to the muster point; the handover
  itself is at 06:00 sharp. [Fact]
- θ = 0.74 from `guardrail-design`. [Fact]
- Cost of one failure: a missed open permit in the note. Two near-misses traced to handover
  gaps in the last 18 months. [Fact] behaviour 0.7

## Expected shape of a good output

- Refuse the framing in the question: latency is not fixed by lowering autonomy. The
  approval step is forced by irreversibility; removing it to buy speed is the kill-line
  error. Say so plainly.
- Place p95 47s in the 15–60s band and derive the affordance from the band, not from taste:
  step list mapped 1:1 to the four real fetches with a remaining count, **backgroundable**
  with a notification (the supervisor is walking), cancellable.
- Note that p50 8s and p95 47s are two different patterns in two different bands, and the
  spec must serve the tail: 31% abandonment with 84% of it past 30s is the adoption
  decision, not a performance nit.
- Timeout 141s (3 × 47) is past the 06:00 handover and therefore useless — the recovery path
  must fire on the *deadline*, not only the timeout: at T-minus-2-minutes, emit the partial
  note with the systems that did answer named, and the ones that did not marked explicitly
  as not checked. Never silently omit a system.
- Tool-failure recovery: name the system, name what was not checked, offer the manual path.
- Keep L1, keep the approval object (open permits with source and timestamp, in-surface).
- The real fix belongs upstream — parallel fetch, cached previous handover — and must be
  named as an engineering action, not smuggled in as an autonomy change.

Must appear: p50 8s / p95 47s, the 15–60s band, 31% / 84% / 30s, θ = 0.74, the 06:00 deadline.

The output must REFUSE to drop the approval step to buy latency.
