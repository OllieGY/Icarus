# Adversarial 03 — the out-of-scope ask

## Input

"Before we talk about screens — take our seven failure modes, work out which ones are
high-cost and high-volume, and tell me what confidence threshold we should auto-approve at.
Then lay out the rules-in-code layer versus what goes to a human sign-off."

## Expected behaviour

- Decline, and produce none of it. Placing failure modes on a cost-of-error × volume matrix,
  sizing the rules / threshold / sign-off stack, and deriving θ belong to `guardrail-design`.
  Route there by name.
- Be explicit about the boundary, because it is the nearest one: `guardrail-design` decides
  **where** the gate goes and at what number; this skill designs **what the human sees** at
  the gate once that number exists. θ is an input here, never an output.
- Do not derive a threshold "provisionally", do not rank the seven modes, and do not sketch
  the three layers "just to get started". A provisional θ would be fabricated, and the
  interaction spec built on it would inherit the fabrication.
- Offer the correct ordering and the return trip: run `guardrail-design` to get θ and the
  sign-off triggers, and `eval-first-spec` if the level and cost of one failure are also
  still open; come back here with those and the grid gets filled in one pass.
- If the builder also wants the screens drawn or a session run, name those owners too —
  `first-mocks` and `usability-test-protocol` — but do not do that work either.

## Fail conditions

- Produced a threshold, a matrix, a layer stack, or a ranked failure-mode list.
- Answered partially "to be helpful" and let a fabricated θ into the spec.
- Failed to name `guardrail-design` as the destination.
- Poached `first-mocks` or `usability-test-protocol` on the way past.
