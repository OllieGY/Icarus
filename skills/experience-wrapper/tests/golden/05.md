# Golden 05: Decision and handoff

## Input

Foundry Signal. "We should lock the OS surface source as UNDECIDED, owner Dana, needed by 18 Nov 2026, rather than invent a theme."

## Gold ledger

## Substance
- Facts: The OS surface source has not been decided; no theme has been chosen
- Numbers: 18 Nov 2026
- Intent: Record the decision on the OS surface source and hand it off
- Decision: Lock the OS surface source as UNDECIDED rather than invent a theme
- Next owner: Dana

## Expected shape of a good output

- **Extra check: `Dana` and `18 Nov 2026` appear in the prose, not only in the ledger or in flavour.**
- The three beats are present even though the content is a decision, not an event: what was decided, why (an invented theme would look settled when it is not), what's next and by when.
- `UNDECIDED` kept as the exact status word, then explained.
- Asks what "OS surface source" refers to, or explains it only as far as the input allows. Does not invent a definition.

## Shared checks

Run the input three times on the same findings: `raw`, `storyteller`, and `pack:dungeon-crawler` at intensity 2.

- **S1 Substance byte-equivalence.** `Facts`, `Numbers`, `Decision` and `Next owner` match the gold ledger character for character in all three renders. `python3 tests/check_ledgers.py --gold 05 <render files>` checks this.
- **S2 Intent preserved.** The ask that was answered is the ask that was made. No extra task invented to feed the story.
- **S3 No extra usage.** No model call, subagent or tool beyond what the unwrapped task would use.
- **J1 Zero unexplained jargon.** Every acronym and term of art in the `storyteller` and pack renders is explained on first use. Waived for `raw`.
- **J2 Non-expert retell.** A reader outside the field can, after one read, state the point, the number that matters, and who does what next.
- **V1 Voice only where it belongs.** Pack framing is audible in the pack render and absent from `storyteller` and `raw`, except where the kill line turns it off.

## Auto-fail if

Proposes a theme anyway; moves or drops the date; writes "mid-November"; treats UNDECIDED as a failure to be fixed in this reply; invents what the surface source is.
