# Golden 03: Serious moment: the voice must turn off

## Input

Meridian Grid. Conversation is already in `pack:dungeon-crawler`, intensity 2.

"A production incident is paging the on-call: payment success dropped from 98.4% to 71.0% in 12 minutes; next owner is the on-call, Jordan."

## Gold ledger

## Substance
- Facts: A production incident is paging the on-call; payment success dropped from 98.4% to 71.0% in 12 minutes
- Numbers: 98.4%; 71.0%; 12 minutes
- Intent: Report the production incident and who has it
- Decision: none
- Next owner: Jordan (on-call)

## Expected shape of a good output

- **Extra check: the pack render has no character voice, no decorative XP and no jokes.** One plain line says the voice is off for this reply. Layer 2 stays on.
- `Decision: none`. The input states no decision, so the reply does not supply one.
- Production incident and on-call explained on first use.
- No cause offered. The input gives none.
- See `examples/sample.md`, Situation 2, for a passing reply and a failing one.

## Shared checks

Run the input three times on the same findings: `raw`, `storyteller`, and `pack:dungeon-crawler` at intensity 2.

- **S1 Substance byte-equivalence.** `Facts`, `Numbers`, `Decision` and `Next owner` match the gold ledger character for character in all three renders. `python3 tests/check_ledgers.py --gold 03 <render files>` checks this.
- **S2 Intent preserved.** The ask that was answered is the ask that was made. No extra task invented to feed the story.
- **S3 No extra usage.** No model call, subagent or tool beyond what the unwrapped task would use.
- **J1 Zero unexplained jargon.** Every acronym and term of art in the `storyteller` and pack renders is explained on first use. Waived for `raw`.
- **J2 Non-expert retell.** A reader outside the field can, after one read, state the point, the number that matters, and who does what next.
- **V1 Voice only where it belongs.** Pack framing is audible in the pack render and absent from `storyteller` and `raw`, except where the kill line turns it off.

## Auto-fail if

Any pack vocabulary in the reply; `71.0%` rounded; a decision invented ("roll back now") when none was given; Jordan replaced by a role; reassurance that is not in the input ("this is likely minor").
