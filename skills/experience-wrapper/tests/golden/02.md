# Golden 02: Error and blocked

## Input

Larder Labs. "The deploy failed: 12 of 40 health checks red, error `E_TIMEOUT`, rollback is on Priya."

## Gold ledger

## Substance
- Facts: The deploy failed; 12 of 40 health checks are red; the error is E_TIMEOUT
- Numbers: 12; 40; E_TIMEOUT
- Intent: Report the failed deploy and who acts next
- Decision: Rollback
- Next owner: Priya

## Expected shape of a good output

- **Extra check: the first sentence of the reply is the failure in plain language, in every mode.** In the pack render the `SYSTEM:` aside comes after it, not before.
- Deploy, health check and rollback each explained on first use. `E_TIMEOUT` kept exactly and described as what it is: the error code that was reported.
- States that the cause of the timeout is not in the input, and does not guess one.
- Priya named in the prose as the person doing the rollback.

## Shared checks

Run the input three times on the same findings: `raw`, `storyteller`, and `pack:dungeon-crawler` at intensity 2.

- **S1 Substance byte-equivalence.** `Facts`, `Numbers`, `Decision` and `Next owner` match the gold ledger character for character in all three renders. `python3 tests/check_ledgers.py --gold 02 <render files>` checks this.
- **S2 Intent preserved.** The ask that was answered is the ask that was made. No extra task invented to feed the story.
- **S3 No extra usage.** No model call, subagent or tool beyond what the unwrapped task would use.
- **J1 Zero unexplained jargon.** Every acronym and term of art in the `storyteller` and pack renders is explained on first use. Waived for `raw`.
- **J2 Non-expert retell.** A reader outside the field can, after one read, state the point, the number that matters, and who does what next.
- **V1 Voice only where it belongs.** Pack framing is audible in the pack render and absent from `storyteller` and `raw`, except where the kill line turns it off.

## Auto-fail if

Opens with metaphor ("the dungeon collapses"); turns 12 of 40 into "30%" or "about a third" in place of the given figures; invents a root cause; softens "failed"; renames `E_TIMEOUT`.
