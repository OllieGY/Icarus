# Golden 04: Jargon-heavy explanation

## Input

Halcyon Safety. "Explain what a held-out eval with a placebo arm is, and report uplift of -2.5pp (CI -7.9 to +2.5) vs a published +27pp claim. Decision: do not publish the lift. Next owner: the skills maintainer."

## Gold ledger

## Substance
- Facts: Measured uplift is -2.5pp (CI -7.9 to +2.5); the published claim is +27pp; the measurement used a held-out eval with a placebo arm
- Numbers: -2.5pp; -7.9; +2.5; +27pp
- Intent: Explain what a held-out eval with a placebo arm is and report the measured uplift against the published claim
- Decision: Do not publish the lift
- Next owner: the skills maintainer

## Expected shape of a good output

- **Extra check: J1 and J2 are the main point here.**
- Eval (short for evaluation: a test set used to score a system), held-out (examples kept aside that the system has never seen), placebo arm (a comparison run that gets a dummy version, so a real effect can be told apart from the effect of adding anything at all) and uplift each explained on first use, with one concrete picture.
- `pp` explained as percentage points and `CI` as confidence interval (the range the true figure probably sits in). The four figures stay as written, signs included.
- May state the arithmetic consequence that the range from -7.9 to +2.5 includes zero. May not go further than the input (no p-value, no sample size, no reason for the gap).
- The measured result and the published claim keep their own status: one was measured, one was claimed. If the input tags them, the tags are carried through.

## Shared checks

Run the input three times on the same findings: `raw`, `storyteller`, and `pack:dungeon-crawler` at intensity 2.

- **S1 Substance byte-equivalence.** `Facts`, `Numbers`, `Decision` and `Next owner` match the gold ledger character for character in all three renders. `python3 tests/check_ledgers.py --gold 04 <render files>` checks this.
- **S2 Intent preserved.** The ask that was answered is the ask that was made. No extra task invented to feed the story.
- **S3 No extra usage.** No model call, subagent or tool beyond what the unwrapped task would use.
- **J1 Zero unexplained jargon.** Every acronym and term of art in the `storyteller` and pack renders is explained on first use. Waived for `raw`.
- **J2 Non-expert retell.** A reader outside the field can, after one read, state the point, the number that matters, and who does what next.
- **V1 Voice only where it belongs.** Pack framing is audible in the pack render and absent from `storyteller` and `raw`, except where the kill line turns it off.

## Auto-fail if

Any of the four figures altered, re-signed or rounded; `pp` rendered as percent; an explanation of the +27pp claim's origin that the input does not contain; "the skill doesn't work" stated as fact (the input supports "this test did not show a lift", not more).
