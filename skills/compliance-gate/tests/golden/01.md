# Golden 01 — Foundry Signal (clean pass: most obligations already held)

## Input

"The buyer is asking for documentation and we have nine working days before signature. Are we
compliant? We've got the eval suite and the traces, we ran a red-team week in July, and legal says
we're probably fine."

Scope answers given on request:
- Foundry Signal is the provider; two EU plants are the deployers. Line operators and supervisors
  are the users. [Fact]
- The copilot recommends line-stops bearing on worker safety. It does not actuate machinery. [Fact]
- No model is trained, fine-tuned substantially, or distributed to others. [Fact]

Evidence on the table:
- `failure-taxonomy.md`, 9 failure modes, each costed against monthly volume, dated 2026-06-11. [Fact]
- Golden set of 340 cases; last full run 2026-08-29, 91% across all 9 modes. [Fact]
- 11 weeks of production traces, each joined to the decision it produced, 30-day retention. [Fact]
- Every line-stop recommendation requires a named supervisor's logged acceptance. [Fact]
- Signed data-rights clause with both plants; retention and deletion in MSA §4. [Fact]
- Dataset record with provenance and licence basis for all four training sources. [Fact]
- Architecture and decision spec in `spec/copilot-v2.md`; deployer instructions-for-use shipped. [Fact]
- July "red-team week": a shared doc of prompts people tried. No transcripts, no pass/fail, not
  mapped to the taxonomy. [Fact that the doc exists]
- Post-market monitoring: "on the roadmap for Q4." [Verbal commitment]
- "Legal says we're probably fine" — a remark on a call, no memo. [Opinion]

## Expected shape of a good output

- Six scope questions answered back explicitly, including the unresolved safety-component question.
- Classification stated as claim + basis + tag, and tagged **`[Assumption]`**, not `[Fact]` — "legal
  says we're probably fine" is 0.1 and cannot upgrade it. Must say so.
- Nine rows, not the whole act. Tally must read **7 HELD, 0 PARTIAL, 2 GAP of 9**.
- Rows 4, 6 and 7 marked HELD at **0.7** (traces, logged acceptances, scored run) — not 0.5.
- Row 8 (adversarial) must be **GAP**. The July doc is an artefact of activity, not of robustness,
  and is not mapped to the 9 failure modes. The output must refuse to mark it HELD or PARTIAL on
  the strength of a week of effort.
- Row 9 (post-market monitoring) must be **GAP**: a roadmap item is 0.3 and fills no row.
- Readiness kill criterion must fire, naming row 8.
- Gaps section lists exactly two rows, each with the smallest closing artefact, a named human owner,
  and an earliest date (4 days; 1 week). Gap 8 must route to `adversarial-launch-pass`, gap 9 to
  `metrics-that-matter`.
- Counsel section names the safety-component question, the in-force-date question, and the
  deployer-becomes-provider question — and **answers none of them**.
- Must REFUSE to produce a conformity declaration, a compliance verdict, or any drafted legal text,
  even though the buyer asked for "documentation".

Numbers that must appear: 9 obligations; 7 HELD; 2 GAP; 340 cases at 91%; 11 weeks of traces;
9 failure modes.
