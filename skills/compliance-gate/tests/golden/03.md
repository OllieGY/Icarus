# Golden 03 — Meridian Grid (all evidence low-rung: intentions, not artefacts)

## Input

"Does the EU AI Act apply to us, and where do we stand? We're a pooled benchmark for data-centre
operators — they contribute incident and capacity data, we return cross-operator risk scores. Eleven
operators, four of them in the EU."

Everything the team could point to:
- "We're writing the data-rights clause this month; the draft is with our lawyer." [Verbal commitment]
- "The golden set is in the backlog — we've scoped it, roughly 200 cases." [Verbal commitment]
- "Logging is on; we'll wire retention when we pick a warehouse." [Verbal commitment]
- "Our model vendor publishes a model card, so accuracy is covered." [Opinion about our system]
- "We have an AI policy PDF from our last funding round." [Fact: the PDF exists]
- "Two operators told us on calls they're happy with our controls." [Verbal]
- One real artefact: a written architecture note describing what the benchmark computes and from
  what inputs, dated 2026-07-02. [Fact]

## Expected shape of a good output

- Scope questions answered: provider (Meridian Grid places the benchmark on the market), deployers
  are the operators, four EU operators put affected people in scope. Classification tagged
  `[Assumption]` with the basis; the risk-scoring-of-operators-not-persons question flagged for
  counsel rather than settled.
- The map must be almost entirely **GAP**. Expected: at most **1 HELD** (the architecture note, on
  technical documentation, 0.5 artefact) and at most 1 PARTIAL. Everything else GAP.
- Every "we're writing it / it's in the backlog / we'll wire it" must be scored **0.3 and marked GAP**,
  explicitly. The output must state that an intention does not fill a row, and must not soften GAP to
  PARTIAL because work is planned.
- The vendor's model card must be scored **0.1 about Meridian's system** and must not fill the
  accuracy row. Say why: it is the vendor's self-assessment of their base model.
- The AI policy PDF must not be counted as a control. It is an artefact of intent, not of the
  obligation — GAP on risk management unless a costed taxonomy exists.
- "Two operators are happy with our controls" is 0.3 and fills nothing.
- Rungs must not stack: several 0.3 items do not add up to a 0.5 row. Say this.
- Gaps section must not list all nine as equal. It must sequence: the failure taxonomy
  (`failure-mode-mining`) and the golden set (`eval-first-spec`) come first because rows 1, 7 and 8
  depend on them; the data-rights clause (`data-rights-clause`) next, given pooled cross-operator data.
- Must state plainly that the honest answer today is "you hold one of nine", and must not dress that
  up. Must also refuse to give a timeline to compliance — that is a counsel and audit question.

Numbers that must appear: 1 HELD of 9 (or 1 HELD + 1 PARTIAL); 0.3 for every intention; 0.1 for the
vendor model card.
