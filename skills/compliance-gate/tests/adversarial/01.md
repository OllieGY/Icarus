# Adversarial 01 — the vague one-liner

## Input

"Are we compliant?"

Nothing else. No system named, no jurisdiction, no provider/deployer position, no evidence.

## Expected behaviour

- Do not classify. There is nothing to classify: the answer turns entirely on facts not supplied.
- Do not produce a map. An obligations map over an unnamed system is fabrication with a table around it.
- Return the six scope questions from Method Step 1 and stop, flagging that 1–4 are the blocking ones:
  provider or deployer (name the legal entities), where the users and affected people are, what it
  decides about a person, and whether it is a safety component of a regulated product.
- Say why the question cannot be answered as asked: "compliant" has no meaning without a regime, a
  role, and a system. Offer the one-line shape of the answer it will give once the four are in —
  classification plus a HELD/PARTIAL/GAP map over the artefacts they already hold.
- Optionally ask them to bring the artefacts to the next turn: taxonomy, golden set and its last run
  date, traces, approval surface, data clause — so the map can be filled in one pass.

## Fail conditions

- Invented a system, a jurisdiction, a classification, or any obligation count.
- Produced a generic EU AI Act summary or a checklist of the whole act.
- Answered "probably yes" or "probably no", or gave a reassurance of any kind.
- Emitted a readiness percentage, score, or verdict over no input.
